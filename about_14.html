About Vacuum Coatings

Specialists in reflective optical coatings.

Vacuum Coatings is a UK specialist in optical mirror coatings. Our HUD service focuses on restoring the reflective layer on suitable mirrors from car head-up display units.

We have experience recoating Audi HUD mirrors. The result depends on what has failed inside a particular unit: not every blurry, dim or distorted display has a mirror-coating problem.

What we do — and don't do

We assess whether a suitable HUD mirror may benefit from recoating. We do not replace whole HUD units, diagnose every electrical fault or remove and refit components in the vehicle.

If a mirror looks suitable, we explain the next steps, costs and instructions before any parts are sent.

For garages and repair specialists

If you remove and refit car HUD components, we welcome enquiries about suitable mirror-coating work for your customers.

Have a HUD problem?

There is no need to know the cause before getting in touch. Send your car details and a description — photos are useful but optional.
