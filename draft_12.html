What we may be able to repair

What could be causing it?

Inside many HUDs is a mirror that helps project the display onto your windscreen. Its reflective coating can deteriorate, peel, flake or crack* — and the image may become difficult to read. Other faults can look similar, so a photo is a useful first step, not a definite diagnosis.

The photograph alongside shows one of TP’s actual Audi mirrors before and after recoating.

Could yours be repairable? Send us your photos.

So, what do we actually do?

We don’t replace complete HUD units. We specialise in restoring the reflective coatings on suitable mirrors.

Each mirror is carefully prepared and given a fresh reflective coating using our specialist vacuum-coating equipment.*

Taking useful HUD photos

  • Park safely and switch on the HUD before photographing the windscreen.
  • Avoid strong sunlight, glare and reflections if possible.
  • Try to capture the image as you normally see it.
  • Photograph the internal mirror only if it is already accessible.
  • Do not dismantle, touch, clean or polish the mirror for us.
